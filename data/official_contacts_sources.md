# Where every value in `official_contacts.json` came from

Checked 2026-11-07. Everything below was read off the agency's own website, word for word. Nothing comes from memory or AI.
`url` = the page where the phone number is listed. `source` = the page for `channels` and `neverAsksFor`.

## USCIS
- **Phone 800-375-5283** (TTY 800-767-1833; outside the U.S. 212-620-3418): https://www.uscis.gov/contactcenter
- **Never asks for:** "We do not accept Western Union, MoneyGram, PayPal, Venmo, or gift cards as payment for immigration fees." / "We will never ask you to transfer money to an individual." / "we will never ask you to pay fees to a person on the phone or by email." See https://www.uscis.gov/scams-fraud-and-misconduct/avoid-scams/common-scams
- **Channels:** "USCIS will only contact you through official government channels" / "will not contact you through your personal social media accounts" / "Email addresses from the U.S. government always end in (.gov)." (same page). The contact center page says USCIS may follow up by email or a phone call.

## IRS
- **Phone 800-829-1040** (individuals, 7 a.m.–7 p.m. local time): https://www.irs.gov/help/telephone-assistance. The FTC page below lists the same number.
- **Channels:** "We normally contact you the first time by mail" / "We email only when you opt in" / "We send text messages only when you opt in" / "A social media direct message is never from us." See https://www.irs.gov/help/how-to-know-its-the-irs (updated 22-Oct-2026)
- **Never asks for:** "The IRS and its debt collectors won't demand that you wire money or pay with gift cards…" / "No government agency will demand you wire money or pay with gift cards, cryptocurrency, or a payment app." See https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-government-impersonator-scam. Prepaid debit cards: "Call to demand immediate payment using a specific payment method such as a prepaid debit card, gift card or wire transfer." See https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/how-to-know-its-really-the-irs-calling-or-knocking-on-your-door (**archived 2017 fact sheet**)

## ICE
- **Phone 866-347-2423**, ICE tip line ("U.S. and Canada: (866) 347-2423", also written 1-866-DHS-2-ICE): https://www.ice.gov/contact
  - Same page also lists the ERO Detention, Removals and Information Line: 1-888-351-4024. Pick this one instead if the team prefers "check if ICE really contacted you" over "report a scam".
- **Never asks for:** "HSI will never call and demand account information or payment over the phone to avoid an impending law enforcement action." / "HSI will not contact you by phone for your bank information or cash." / "HSI won't call to threaten you with jail time and demand money in lieu of jail." See https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/hsi-michigan-and-ohio-warn-scammers-impersonating-special-agents (**marked archived**, Oct 2024)
- **Channels:** left empty. No ice.gov page lists how ICE contacts people.

## FTC
- **Phone 877-382-4357** ("1-877-FTC-HELP (382-4357)") and **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**: https://www.ftc.gov/contact
- **Never asks for:** "The FTC will never ask you for money, threaten to arrest you, or promise you a prize." / never "tell you to withdraw cash or buy gold and give it to someone." See https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-government-impersonator-scam (Nov 2023)
- **Channels:** left empty. The FTC is where you report scams; it doesn't contact you about payments.

## SSA
- **Phone: NOT FILLED IN (`null`).** ssa.gov blocks automated reading, and the SSA OIG pages don't list a phone number. **To do by hand:** open https://www.ssa.gov/agency/contact/ in a browser, copy the main toll-free number, and put it in the `phone` field.
- **Never asks for:** "No legitimate government employee will ever … Ask for gift cards, gold bars, cryptocurrency, wire transfer, or cash." See https://oig.ssa.gov/scam-alerts/2026-04-28-new-wave-of-imposter-scams-criminals-using-real-ssa-employee-names-and-fake-badges/ (Apr 2026). Prepaid debit: SSA will never "require payment by retail gift card, cash, wire transfer, internet currency, or prepaid debit card". See https://oig.ssa.gov/scam-alerts/2025-07-17-new-twist-to-social-security-phone-scams/
- **Channels:** "in most cases Social Security will mail you a letter" (same 2025 page)
- **url:** "oig.ssa.gov/report" is the SSA OIG's scam-report address, quoted from the 2026 alert.
